# Harmonic Arcana

Ties the 22 tarot trumps to musical notes, so a reading can be heard and turned into music.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It is one file with nothing to install. Everything you save stays in that browser on that device.

- **The Tree of Life is the instrument.** Each of the 22 paths is a card and a note. Tap a letter or its line to hear it, drag sideways to strum, tap a sphere to hear the paths that meet in it.
- **Draw a reading** (Single, Past/Present/Future, Four Elements, Celtic Cross). It lights its route on the tree and can be played as a chord, an arpeggio, or as a piece three ways: Singing bowls, Night drive, Overtone drone.
- **MIDI out** (Settings, at the foot of the page) sends everything to a synth or a DAW. It needs Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer; iPhone and iPad Safari do not have it.
- **It leaves the screen:** a MIDI file of a reading, a printable reading sheet with the notes on a staff, and a line drawing of the tree with the reading on it.

This file is built and checked in `Domnoval/Tool-collection-and-improvements-` (under `tools/harmonic-arcana/`, with its record in `ledger/harmonic-arcana/`). Changes are made there and sent here.
