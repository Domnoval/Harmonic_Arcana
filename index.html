Touch a letter or its path to hear the card. Drag sideways across the paths to strum (on a phone a note sounds when you lift your finger, so scrolling stays silent). Touch a sphere to hear the paths that meet in it. On a keyboard: Tab to the tree, arrows to move, Enter to play.

Lay my own cards
Shuffle the deck yourself

One deck of 78 lies here and keeps its order between visits. Shuffle it as you would in your hands; the next spread is dealt from the top, as the deck lies. A card comes out reversed only if you have turned part of the deck.

Keys

Keyboard: Z S X D C V G B H N J M play C4 to B4 · Q 2 W 3 E R 5 T 6 Y 7 U play C5 to B5 · Esc stops. Not while Ctrl, Alt or Cmd is held or a field is focused.
